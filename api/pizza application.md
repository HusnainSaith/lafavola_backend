# **Pizza Restaurant Mobile Application – Functional Requirements Document**

## **1\. Project Overview**

The Pizza Restaurant Mobile Application is designed to provide customers with a simple, fast, and convenient way to order pizzas and other menu items. The application allows customers to browse the complete menu, customize their orders, make secure payments, track deliveries in real time, and manage their personal profiles.

The main objective of the application is to enhance customer experience while simplifying the restaurant's ordering and delivery process.

# **2\. User Registration and Login**

Customers can create an account using:

* Email address  
* Mobile phone number  
* Password

Alternatively, they can log in using:

* Existing account credentials  
* Google or Apple login (optional)

After logging in, customers can access their profile, previous orders, saved addresses, and payment preferences.

# **3\. Customer Profile**

Each customer has a personal profile containing:

* Full Name  
* Phone Number  
* Email Address  
* Delivery Address  
* Additional Delivery Instructions  
* Saved Payment Methods (optional)  
* Order History  
* Favorite Orders  
* Loyalty Points (optional)

Customers can update their personal information at any time.

# **4\. Complete Menu**

The application displays a complete digital menu.

Each menu item includes:

* Pizza Name  
* High-quality Image  
* Description  
* List of Ingredients  
* Pizza Size  
  * Small  
  * Medium  
  * Large  
* Base Price  
* Calories (optional)  
* Preparation Time

Example:

### **Margherita Pizza**

Ingredients:

* Tomato Sauce  
* Mozzarella Cheese  
* Fresh Basil

Base Price:

* Small – $8.99

# **5\. Pizza Categories**

The application contains three ordering categories.

## **Category 1 – Standard Menu Pizza**

Customers simply select a pizza from the menu.

Example:

* Pepperoni Pizza  
* Hawaiian Pizza  
* BBQ Chicken Pizza  
* Veggie Pizza

No modifications are required.

The displayed price is the final base price.

## **Category 2 – Slightly Modified Pizza**

Customers select a pizza from the menu and make small modifications.

Examples include:

* Extra Cheese  
* Remove Onion  
* Remove Olives  
* Add Jalapeños  
* Thin Crust  
* Stuffed Crust

Each additional ingredient has an extra cost.

Example:

Margherita Pizza  
Base Price: $10.99

Extra Cheese: \+$1.50

Extra Mushrooms: \+$1.00

Stuffed Crust: \+$2.50

Final Price:  
$15.99

The application automatically updates the total price after each modification.

* Medium  
* Large

* Thin Crust  
* Regular Crust  
* Pan Pizza  
* Stuffed Crust

Step 3 – Choose Sauce

* Tomato Sauce  
* BBQ Sauce

* White Sauce  
* Pesto Sauce

Step 4 – Choose Cheese

* Mozzarella  
* Cheddar

Step 5 – Select Toppings

Vegetables:

* Onion

* Pepperoni

# **8\. Payment Options**

Customers can choose between two payment methods.

## **Option 1 – Pay Through the App**

* Credit Card  
* Debit Card  
* Apple Pay  
* Google Pay  
* Digital Wallets

**Option 2 – Pay Upon Delivery**

* Cash  
* Credit Card

# **20\. Summary**

The Pizza Restaurant Mobile Application offers a complete digital ordering experience. Customers can browse a detailed menu with ingredients and prices, choose from standard pizzas, slightly modified pizzas, or fully customized pizzas with automatically calculated extra charges. They can pay securely through the app or upon delivery using cash or card, track their order with a live countdown from the estimated 30-minute delivery time, access exclusive promotions and deals, and manage their profile, addresses, and order history. The system also provides restaurant administrators with powerful tools for managing menus, pricing, promotions, orders, and deliveries, creating an efficient and customer-friendly pizza ordering platform.
